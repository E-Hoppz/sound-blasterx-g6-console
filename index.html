Sound BlasterX G6 Studio Console

An ultra-lightweight, zero-bloat live mixer, safety limiter, and hardware controller engineered specifically for the Creative Labs SB1770.

"Dedicated exclusively to Creative Labs hardware. The CS43131 MasterHIFI DAC, WIMA film capacitors, and discrete Xamp bi-amp represent great audio engineering. This tool provides the pure, lightweight software they deserve."

Live 3-Channel Mixer

Dedicated gain staging and faders for Mic In, Line/Optical combo receiver, and What U Hear digital loopback.

Ear & Monitor Safety Limiter

Live 3-band parametric EQ and a brickwall safety compressor/limiter to protect against deafening digital overs.

Schematic-Accurate Presets

Component-accurate emulations of Sound Blaster Live! Pro, AWE64 Gold, Audigy 2 ZS, and X-Fi Fatal1ty.

Raw USB HID LED Control

Direct low-level USB commands to control the RGB 'X' logo (Custom RGB, Amber, Cyan, Green, Stealth OFF) with 0ms lag.

Zero Telemetry, Zero Services

No analytics DLLs, no background daemons, no memory leaks into audiodg.exe. Fixes the crunchy sound bug forever.

1-Click Equalizer APO Sync

Export your mixer curves into system-level audio instantly with sub-millisecond latency.

Hardware Architecture Verified

  • Primary DAC Cirrus Logic CS43131 MasterHIFI (32-bit / 384kHz, 130dB DNR)
  • Analog LPF Reconstruction Audiophile German WIMA Film/Foil Capacitors
  • Headphone Stage Discrete Dual-Mono Push-Pull Xamp (1.0 Ω Output Impedance)
  • Primary ADC Cirrus Logic CS5361 Multi-Bit Delta-Sigma (114dB DNR)
  • Output Path Switching Takamisawa Mechanical Telecom-Grade Relay
  • Gain Switch (Low / High) 100% Analog Hardware DPDT Feedback Resistor Switch
